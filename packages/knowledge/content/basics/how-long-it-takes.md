Nobody can promise a date for a first lucid dream. What the research offers is a set of ranges from very different kinds of study, and they should be read as ranges, not as a schedule.

## Where people start

Before any training, lucid dreams are not rare. A quality-effects meta-analysis of 50 years of research found that 55 percent of people have had at least one, and 23 percent have them once a month or more [B32]. Someone who already has monthly lucid dreams will respond to a technique differently from someone who has never had one, so your own starting point matters more than any average.

## What the programmes assume

- **Two weeks.** The Australian and international induction studies ran for two weeks: one week of logbook only, then one week of techniques such as MILD or SSILD after an alarm at about five hours [D11][D12].
- **Three weeks a unit.** The Lucidity Institute course allows at least three weeks for each unit [D5].
- **Four to five weeks.** Tholey claimed a median of four to five weeks to a first lucid dream with his combined technique practised daily, but that figure is his own and uncontrolled [D1][D6].
- **Eight weeks and more.** The app's course has eight lessons, with in-dream skills last.

## What counts as success

In field studies of MILD, lucid dreams came on roughly 17 to 20 percent of nights [B37][B38]. That is about one night in five or six, not every night. Reviews also warn that the quality of the evidence is low and no technique has been shown to work reliably for everyone [B34]. Aspy's 2020 paper cites a study that found at most 6 percent lucid days with Tholey's combined technique [D12].

## Dry spells are normal

The literature treats dry spells as expected. LaBerge's remedies are rest, renewed motivation, and a lower-effort method when concentration is low [D1][D5]. There is more on this in [Common mistakes and dry spells](/learn/basics/common-mistakes-and-dry-spells).

## A fair way to measure yourself

Count lucid nights over weeks, not single nights, and compare with your own earlier weeks rather than with other people.
