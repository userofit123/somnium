## What to do

Decide before sleep what you want to do in your next lucid dream, and keep it simple.

- Use expectation. LaBerge found that what he expected decided what happened, and advises starting small: change one detail of the scene, then more, treating it as "modeling clay for the mind" [D1].
- Ask the dream. Tholey found that addressing figures changes them: asking a stranger "Who are you?" often turned them into someone familiar [D8].
- Face a nightmare instead of escaping it. Look the threatening figure in the eye and open a dialogue: "Who are you?", "What do you want from me?", "Can I help you?" [D1][D10]. Tholey found that conciliatory dialogue worked best, and that attacking a figure tended to bring back avengers [D8][D10].
- Practise a skill. Plan the movement while awake and rehearse it in the dream [B65].

The traditions offer another use: Tibetan dream yoga treats the dream as a place to train awareness, starting by day with the thought "This is a dream" held with real presence [D28][D30]. The app presents these as traditions, not as evidence.

## Why

Control comes later than lucidity: in a Brazilian sample 77 percent had been lucid but only 29 percent had controlled a dream [B31]. Most lucid dreamers use it for fun, then nightmares, problem solving, creativity and practising skills [B67]. For nightmares the studies are small but mostly positive [B58][B62]; imagery rehearsal while awake remains the better-proven treatment, so see a therapist for nightmares that come with trauma [B61]. Practice in a lucid dream improved a coin-tossing task and finger tapping in small studies, without harming sleep [B64][B65]. People also feel more refreshed after lucid nights, not less [B68].

## Today's practice

- Chose one simple thing to do in your next lucid dream
- Rehearsed it while awake, imagining it as vividly as you can
- For a recurring bad dream: practised the opening questions in an imagined dialogue

## Ready to move on

After 2 more lucid dreams, or after 4 weeks. You can move on sooner whenever you like.
