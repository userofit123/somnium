## Origin

Daniel Love, United Kingdom. The text is marked "(c) 2004 Daniel Love", circulated on LD4all and later printed in full in his book Are You Dreaming? (2013) [D24].

## Evidence level

Anecdotal (originator's reports). Love states that in his experience and with his students "8 of 10" reported at least one lucid dream (about two on average) within two weeks, that the method yields at best about four long clear lucid dreams a week, and that it "has not been researched widely enough for a definitive success rate" [D24]. There is no independent study.

## Steps

Stage 1, cycle adjustment, 7 days [D24]:

1. Keep a fixed bedtime and wake time.
2. Set the alarm 90 minutes before your normal wake time. Each night set the intention to wake at the adjusted time.
3. When the alarm goes off, get up and stay up.
4. For the next 90 minutes do ordinary activities and perform a reality test every 2 to 5 minutes; a timer helps.
5. Carry on with the day; daytime reality tests are optional. Repeat every day for the week.

Stage 2, ongoing induction, alternating days [D24]:

1. Day A: set the intention to reality test at the earlier time, but set the alarm for your normal wake time. The body, expecting to wake early, is in a long REM period at that time. If you wake naturally at the early time, stay still and try to induce a lucid dream.
2. Day B: repeat the Stage 1 routine: wake 90 minutes early, stay up, reality test for 90 minutes.
3. Alternate A and B without missing days. If you stop, restart from Stage 1. Love says strict adherence is vital and suggests refreshing the cycle with Stage 1 every few months.

## When

Only when you can keep a strict schedule for weeks. The method relies on the same fact as wake-back-to-bed and the nap protocols, that REM periods are longest late in the sleep period [B2][D4], but it trades sleep continuity for timing.

## Common errors

Sleeping through the early alarm; irregular schedules; disturbing a bed partner; sleep loss. Love's design deliberately trades sleep continuity for timing, so it is unsuitable for anyone who cannot keep the schedule [D24].

## A caution

The Day B routine shortens sleep by 90 minutes every other day. The safety guidance in this app says never to shorten total sleep to make room for practice and to stop if daytime sleepiness, mood or concentration suffer [B69]. If you try CAT, treat those rules as overriding it.
