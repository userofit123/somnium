## What this shows

Your answers to the two questions in Learn · Where am I, every time you answered, whatever the period. Each answer is turned into a count, such as lucid dreams a month, so the line can show a trend.

## How to read it

The questions are the lucid dream frequency scale and the dream recall question that researchers use; answered twice a few weeks apart, people give much the same answer [B49, B50]. So the questions are steady enough to follow over months.

## What to do

Answer them again every month or two: [Where am I](/learn/where-am-i).
