## What this shows

Your answers to the two self-assessment questions, every time you answered, whatever the period. Each answer is turned into a count, such as lucid dreams a month, so the line can show a trend.

## How to read it

The questions are the lucid dream frequency scale and the dream recall question that researchers use; answered twice a few weeks apart, people give much the same answer [B49, B50]. So the questions are steady enough to follow over months.

## What to do

Answer them again every four weeks or so: [Self-assessment](/progress/self-assessment). Beside your first and latest answer is what your journal logged in the four weeks before it. A journal usually finds more dreams than a question about your habits does, because writing them down helps you remember them and a single question tends to guess low [B48, C28a]; so set the two side by side rather than expecting them to match.
