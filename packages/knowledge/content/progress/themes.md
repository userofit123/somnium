## What this shows

The bar is the share of your dreams that touch each theme, found on this device from your words and the themes you added. It shows once you have {THEMES_MIN_DREAMS} dreams. On a large journal only the newest dreams are read here.

The line under it is a different measure: the share of 1,181 Canadian students who said they had ever had the theme at some point in their life [C13].

## How to read it

A theme most people have had can still be in few of their dreams, so a share well under theirs is normal. What does compare is the order: the common themes are much the same from one group of people to the next [C13, C14, C9].

## What to do

A theme that keeps coming back is a dream sign to watch for [C1]: [Dream signs](/signs).
