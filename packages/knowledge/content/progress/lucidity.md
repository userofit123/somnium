## What this shows

**Lucid dreams** counts every dream you marked lucid in the period. **Nights with one** is nights with at least one lucid dream out of the nights you logged, the measure the induction studies use [D12]. **Of all dreams** is lucid dreams out of all your dreams.

The level (1 to 5) and the control are your own ratings. Lucidity is a range, not a yes or no: people describe everything from a brief flash of knowing to full clarity, and the scale researchers use grades insight and control apart [B79, B22].

The lower tiles and the two lines read every lucid dream since your first, whatever the period: the last 3 months against the 3 months before, once {QUALITY_MIN} lucid dreams on a side have a rating.

## How to read it

In a study with a week of logging and then a week of practice, the group doing reality checks, WBTB and MILD went from lucid dreams on 9.4% of nights to 17.4% [D11]. In the largest study, SSILD and MILD after WBTB each gave lucid dreams on about 17% of nights [D12]. Across 34 surveys, nearly 1 in 4 has a lucid dream once a month or more [B32].

With few lucid dreams, one more or one less moves a percentage a lot, so read the counts under each number [C12a].

## What to do

Before lucid dreams come, keep practising each night: [Plan tonight](/train/tonight). Once they come, the training turns to keeping them going longer [D1]: [Keeping a lucid dream going](/learn/techniques/stabilisation).
