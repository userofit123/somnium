## What this shows

What went with your lucid nights, from your own nights only. For each technique or habit, the bar is the share of nights with a lucid dream on nights you did it, and the grey bar the share on nights you did not.

A rate shows once there are {MIN_NIGHTS} nights on each side. Until then, what each lucid night came after and how many nights you tried each technique show as plain counts.

## How to read it

It shows what went together, not what caused what: a good night can follow anything. A few nights can go either way by chance [C12a], and even the best techniques gave a lucid dream on about 1 night in 6 in the largest study [D12].

In an earlier study, how fast people fell asleep after MILD mattered most: falling asleep within five minutes went with a lucid dream on about 46% of attempts [D11].

## What to do

Log the techniques you did and the minutes it took to fall asleep after them, every night, lucid or not, so both sides fill up: [Plan tonight](/train/tonight).
