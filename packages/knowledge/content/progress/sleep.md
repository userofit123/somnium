## What this shows

Hours asleep run from bedtime to wake time, less the minutes awake for WBTB. **Usual bedtime** is your typical bedtime, and "varies by about" is how far bedtimes usually stray from it. Numbers show once {SLEEP_MIN_NIGHTS} nights have both times; naps are left out.

## How to read it

REM periods grow longer through the night, so the last hours of sleep hold the most REM, and that is where lucid dreams mostly come [C31, B1, C1]. Cutting sleep to train cuts that time [B69, D1].

WBTB breaks sleep up on purpose. In a four-study project, waking in the night went with lucid dreaming while rated sleep quality did not; in two surveys, people with frequent lucid dreams rated their sleep worse [B77, B80]. The WBTB line sets your WBTB nights against the others once each side has {MIN_NIGHTS} nights.

## What to do

Sleep comes first: if you are losing sleep to practice, ease off for a while [D1]. [Sleep first](/learn/safety/sleep-first).
