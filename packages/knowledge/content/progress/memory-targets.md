## What this shows

The targets you set over the last 7 days, whatever the period: how many you caught and how many you missed. A target you set and never logged counts as a miss once its day is over.

## How to read it

This is LaBerge's exercise for remembering to do something later, which he treats as a skill to build before MILD, alongside good dream recall [D1].

## What to do

Keep going for at least a week; if you still miss most targets, carry on until you hit most of them [D1]. [Memory targets](/learn/techniques/prospective-memory).
