## What this shows

Every reality check you logged in the period: how many a day on average, which kind, what each one said, and how many came from a reminder rather than on your own. **By dream sign** counts checks a reminder paired with one of your dream signs.

## How to read it

More is not better here. Daily reality checks on their own did not raise lucid dreams within a week in two field studies [D11, D12]. Read the numbers as how well the habit is going, not as a forecast. What the checks aim at is noticing, in a dream, a sign you already know [C1, C2].

## What to do

Do fewer checks and mean each one: check when something odd or dreamlike happens, and ask what it would be like if this were a dream. A check done on autopilot trains the same "obviously I'm awake" in the dream [D1, D8, D11, D20]. [Reality testing](/learn/techniques/reality-testing).
