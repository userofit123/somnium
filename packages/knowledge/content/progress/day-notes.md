## What this shows

Which things you wrote about in a day note came back in a dream 1 to 2 days later, or 5 to 7 days later, with the count you would expect by chance beside it. A thing shows once it is named in at least {LAG_MIN_MENTIONS} notes.

## How to read it

These are counts from your own notes, not a test. Studies found the 5 to 7 day delay in some groups and not in others [C23, C48, C24], and things you write about are often the things you keep thinking about, so a match does not show cause.

## What to do

Keep writing a short note most days, naming the people, places and things in it, so the counts have something to go on: [How the app finds signs](/learn/dream-signs/how-the-app-finds-signs).
