---
title: Why 5 to 6 hours, then up for a while
reviewed: 2026-11-15
sources: [B2, B3, D13, D14]
more: techniques/wbtb
---
Dream sleep gets longer as the night goes on, so waking after 5 to 6 hours puts your practice just before the longest dreams. In the lab, 60 minutes awake worked better than 30, and waking at 4.5 hours worked much less well.
