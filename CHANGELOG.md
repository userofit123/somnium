# Changelog

## Unreleased

A rework of the whole app: every screen has one clear place, one look and one main action, and new help for each
step of learning to dream lucidly.

- **A clearer app.** New phone tabs (Home, Journal, +, Train, Progress, More) and a matching desktop sidebar. One +
  button adds a dream, No recall, a reality check, tonight's plan or a day note. Insights is now Progress, and Train
  has three tabs: Today, Tonight and Techniques. Every page shares one header, Back button, empty state, loading state
  and button set, and Journal, Dream signs, Learn and Settings use one list-and-detail layout on a computer.
- **A first-run setup.** It asks your experience, goal, sleep times, nights you can sleep in, reminders, tonight's
  technique and a short safety check, so day one has a plan.
- **Home that leads your day.** A greeting and today's date, Today's step in the course, a calm This morning card, one
  Today checklist shared with Train, tonight's plan, your streaks and recall, and optional cards you arrange with Edit
  home, such as a dream from your past or Your path.
- **A gentle morning capture.** A dim screen that asks you to lie still and rewind, then key words first; write it
  out now or later. At night, "I woke up" notes the time you woke from a dream.
- **Tonight in one place.** Train · Tonight plans techniques, intention, bedtime, the WBTB alarm and what you will do
  once lucid. It suggests a plan from your lesson and the nights you can sleep in, and the bedtime reminder opens a
  two-minute routine that ends on a dark screen.
- **Reality checks that teach.** Reminders rotate through your top dream signs, and a 15-second guided check asks what
  just happened and what would be different if this were a dream.
- **Learning from your own nights.** After you save a dream the app points out the dream signs in it; your first
  lucid dream gets a calm moment of its own. Progress shows your own early results per technique, recall against when
  you started, lucid dream quality, sleep, checks per dream sign, when in the night your dreams come, Your path of
  milestones and an optional weekly review. Every chart has a note on how to read it, what to do and its sources.
- **Teaching in place.** Short "why this works" notes the first time you try something, calm notes on sleep
  paralysis, false awakenings and nightmares, readings for each lesson, and advice for dry spells and stalls.
- **Night practice on Android.** SSILD, WILD, sound cues and WBTB play soft tones with the screen off.
- **Settings that make sense.** Practice settings come first, reminders and reality checks have their own screens,
  plain names and switches throughout, a Text size setting, and dates and times that follow your device.
- **Safer and steadier.** Everything you change saves offline and syncs later, the setup's health answers stay
  encrypted on your device, and the unused email field is gone.
- **A calmer app lock.** It now locks 10 minutes after you leave the app or switch tabs (you can still choose
  Immediately in Settings → Security), and reloading the page within that time no longer asks for your password.


## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

The first version of Somnium: a private, self-hosted lucid-dreaming journal and trainer for the web, Windows, Linux
and Android.

- **A sealed journal.** Dreams and every personal field are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server
  only ever stores ciphertext and never sees your password. A recovery key, two-factor sign-in with backup codes, and
  a reset code make sure there is always a way back in.
- **App lock everywhere.** Every install asks for your password, fingerprint, face or screen lock on start and when you
  come back to it. After five wrong tries each further try waits longer (1 minute, 5, 15, then an hour), and nothing
  is ever deleted.
- **Journal.** Nights and dreams with lucid, vivid, nightmare, recurring and false-awakening marks, a calendar, search,
  and import from Markdown, plain text and CSV.
- **Found on your device.** Characters, places, objects, actions and feelings are suggested as you write, and photos
  of handwritten pages are read with on-device OCR. Nothing is sent to a cloud service.
- **Signs and Insights.** Recurring dream signs ranked by how often they appear, recall and lucidity over time, and
  what works for you, by technique and by wake-back-to-bed timing.
- **Train.** A technique library with evidence levels, a step-by-step course, tonight's plan with guided MILD and
  wake-back-to-bed modes, a lucid nap planner, sound-cue training, prospective-memory practice and daily habits.
- **Reminders.** Reality-check reminders spread over your day, with your own checks or recommended ones, plus bedtime,
  wake-back-to-bed and morning journal reminders. On Android they use exact alarms; the desktop app keeps them coming
  from the tray.
- **Learn.** A learning hub of 73 sourced articles from history to current research, with a reading order, search,
  read marks, pins, a glossary and an FAQ. Every recommendation cites its sources.
- **Works offline.** The app opens and works with no connection and syncs when you are back online. Two devices that
  logged the same night are merged.
- **Your accounts, your servers.** Keep an account on this device only, or on your own server. Settings → Server lets
  you add, name and switch between servers and local accounts.
- **Backups and export.** Automatic encrypted backups on a schedule, and export to JSON, Markdown and CSV.
- **The same look and options as Money Manager and Motus.** Appearance, colour looks with light and dark modes, the
  status bar, the update check and the same install choices.
