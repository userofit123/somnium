## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

The first version of Somnium: a private, self-hosted lucid-dreaming journal and trainer for the web, Windows, Linux
and Android.

- **A sealed journal.** Dreams and every personal field are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server
  only ever stores ciphertext and never sees your password. A recovery key, two-factor sign-in with backup codes, and
  a reset code make sure there is always a way back in.
- **App lock everywhere.** Every install asks for your password, fingerprint, face or screen lock on start and when you
  come back to it. After five wrong tries each further try waits longer (1 minute, 5, 15, then an hour), and nothing
  is ever deleted.
- **Journal.** Nights and dreams with lucid, vivid, nightmare, recurring and false-awakening marks, a calendar, search,
  and import from Markdown, plain text and CSV.
- **Found on your device.** Characters, places, objects, actions and feelings are suggested as you write, and photos
  of handwritten pages are read with on-device OCR. Nothing is sent to a cloud service.
- **Signs and Insights.** Recurring dream signs ranked by how often they appear, recall and lucidity over time, and
  what works for you, by technique and by wake-back-to-bed timing.
- **Train.** A technique library with evidence levels, a step-by-step course, tonight's plan with guided MILD and
  wake-back-to-bed modes, a lucid nap planner, sound-cue training, prospective-memory practice and daily habits.
- **Reminders.** Reality-check reminders spread over your day, with your own checks or recommended ones, plus bedtime,
  wake-back-to-bed and morning journal reminders. On Android they use exact alarms; the desktop app keeps them coming
  from the tray.
- **Learn.** A learning hub of 73 sourced articles from history to current research, with a reading order, search,
  read marks, pins, a glossary and an FAQ. Every recommendation cites its sources.
- **Works offline.** The app opens and works with no connection and syncs when you are back online. Two devices that
  logged the same night are merged.
- **Your accounts, your servers.** Keep an account on this device only, or on your own server. Settings → Server lets
  you add, name and switch between servers and local accounts.
- **Backups and export.** Automatic encrypted backups on a schedule, and export to JSON, Markdown and CSV.
- **The same look and options as Money Manager and Motus.** Appearance, colour looks with light and dark modes, the
  status bar, the update check and the same install choices.
